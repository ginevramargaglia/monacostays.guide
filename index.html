Monaco, our way.

Not quite a guidebook. More like a local's map.

Monaco is small enough to cross in an afternoon, but there is considerably more to discover than the Casino and the harbour. We have gathered gardens, viewpoints, old streets, quiet corners, architecture and beautiful places worth making time for.

Gardens & Green Spaces

01
01 · Garden

Jardins Saint-Martin

Monaco's first public garden, winding along the cliffs above the Mediterranean. Beautifully quiet in the morning.

02 · Garden

Japanese Garden

A surprisingly peaceful pocket beside the sea, designed around water, stone, bridges and carefully composed greenery.

03

Jardin Exotique

A dramatic cliffside botanical garden filled with succulents and panoramic views.

04

Princess Grace Rose Garden

A peaceful Fontvieille garden dedicated to Princess Grace, with thousands of roses.

05

Parc Princesse Antoinette

A greener, quieter side of Monaco above the main tourist routes.

06

Fontvieille Park

Open green space, palms, sculptures and a slower atmosphere beside the harbour.

07

La Petite Afrique

A lush garden near the Casino, unexpectedly secluded despite its central location.

08

Square Gastaud

A small landscaped pause in the city — perfect when you want somewhere quiet without leaving Monte-Carlo.

Our little tip

The Japanese Garden is one of those places that feels completely removed from Monaco's pace. Give it ten quiet minutes.

By the Sea

02
09 · Beach

Larvotto

Monaco's public beach and waterfront promenade. Go early for a completely different atmosphere.

10 · Hidden corner

Plage des Pêcheurs

A tiny rocky waterfront beneath the old town — one of the places that feels surprisingly far from the city.

11

Port Hercule

Walk the harbour rather than simply looking at it from above.

12

Port de Fontvieille

A calmer harbour to wander around at golden hour.

13

Solarium Rainier III

A distinctive concrete promenade at the foot of the Rock, surrounded by open sea.

14

Monte-Carlo Beach

A classic Riviera setting just beyond Monaco's eastern edge.

15

Grimaldi Forum Waterfront

Walk the seafront beside the Forum for a more contemporary side of Monaco.

16

Le Rocher Waterfront

Follow the water around the western side of the Rock for dramatic views back towards Monaco.

Our little tip

Larvotto before 9am is another place entirely. The water is calm, the promenade is quiet and Monaco has not quite started its day.

A different perspective

Look up. Monaco goes much higher than you think.

One of the pleasures of Monaco is discovering how vertical it is. Take the lifts, climb the small passages and follow the streets above the centre for unexpected views across the rooftops and sea.

The Rock & Monaco-Ville

03
17

Monaco-Ville

Wander without a route. The old streets of the Rock are at their best when you simply follow whichever staircase looks interesting.

18

Place du Palais

The ceremonial heart of Monaco, with views stretching across the rooftops and Mediterranean.

19

Place de la Mairie

A tiny square that captures the quieter, village-like character of Monaco-Ville.

20

Place Saint-Nicolas

A small historic corner to pause before continuing through the old town.

21

Rampe Major

The historic approach to the Rock — best explored slowly.

22

Rue Basse

One of the old town's atmospheric little streets, especially beautiful early in the morning.

23

Rue des Remparts

Follow the edge of the Rock and look for glimpses of the sea between the old buildings.

24

Cathédrale de Monaco

The Romanesque-Byzantine cathedral at the heart of Monaco-Ville.

25

Fort Antoine

An unusual open-air theatre overlooking the sea and one of Monaco's most atmospheric corners.

26

Oceanographic Museum Terraces

Even if you do not visit the museum, the terraces and views over the Mediterranean are worth the walk.

27

Chapelle de la Miséricorde

A small historic chapel tucked into the old town.

Our little tip

Visit Monaco-Ville before the day gets busy. Around 8:30–9:00am, the narrow streets feel almost private.

Art, Architecture & Culture

04
28

Casino de Monte-Carlo

Belle Époque architecture and one of the most recognisable buildings on the Riviera.

29

Salle Garnier

The intimate opera house of Monte-Carlo, built in the same architectural spirit as the Casino.

30

Hôtel de Paris & Place du Casino

One of Monaco's most iconic architectural ensembles — worth seeing even if you simply walk through the square.

31

Villa Paloma

Contemporary art inside one of Monaco's elegant historic villas.

32

Villa Sauber

The Belle Époque home of the Nouveau Musée National de Monaco.

33

Grimaldi Forum

Monaco's contemporary cultural centre for exhibitions, performances and major events.

34

Prince's Car Collection

A fascinating collection for anyone interested in automotive history and Monaco's royal heritage.

35

Chapelle de la Visitation

A small historic space on the Rock with an unexpectedly intimate atmosphere.

36

Auditorium Rainier III

A quieter cultural landmark for concerts and performances.

The Little Discoveries

05
37 · Viewpoint

Tête de Chien

High above Monaco, with one of the Riviera's most extraordinary views back towards the Principality.

38 · Walk

Chemin des Révoires

A quieter climb through Monaco's upper neighbourhoods, with views appearing between the buildings.

39 · Evening

Le Rocher at Sunset

Walk towards the western edge of the Rock and watch the city change colour.

40

Fontvieille Sculpture Trail

A quieter side of Monaco with gardens, sculpture and plenty of space to wander.

41

Public Lifts of Monaco

One of the city's best-kept practical secrets. Use the lifts to move vertically through Monaco.

42

Larvotto at Dawn

Before the beach becomes busy, the promenade is calm, quiet and almost cinematic.

43

La Petite Afrique

A small green pocket beside the Casino that is surprisingly easy to miss.

44

Japanese Garden at Dusk

Return later in the day when the garden becomes quieter and the light softens.

45

Fontvieille Viewpoints

Look back towards the Rock from the western side of Monaco for a different perspective.

The Monaco trick

Don't be afraid of the lifts. Monaco is vertical, and some of the best little discoveries are hidden between its different levels.

Beyond Monaco

06

When you have explored the Principality, the Riviera begins immediately. These are the places we would choose for a half-day escape.

10 minutes

Cap d'Ail

A beautiful coastal village directly beside Monaco. Follow the waterfront and discover the dramatic coastline.

10 minutes

Plage Mala

A spectacular little cove beneath the cliffs of Cap d'Ail.

15 minutes

Roquebrune-Cap-Martin

Medieval streets, sea views and a completely different atmosphere just east of Monaco.

15 minutes

Beaulieu-sur-Mer

Elegant Riviera architecture, palms and beautiful waterfront walks.

20 minutes

Saint-Jean-Cap-Ferrat

One of the most beautiful peninsulas on the Côte d'Azur, especially for a slow afternoon by the sea.

20 minutes

Villefranche-sur-Mer

A postcard-perfect bay with colourful façades and a wonderful waterfront promenade.

25 minutes

Èze Village

Medieval stone streets perched high above the Mediterranean.

25 minutes

Èze-sur-Mer

Leave the village above and head down towards the sea for a completely different side of Èze.

30 minutes

Menton

Pastel façades, gardens and a distinctly Italian feeling at the eastern edge of the Riviera.

30 minutes

Nice

The Promenade des Anglais, Old Town and extraordinary views from Castle Hill.

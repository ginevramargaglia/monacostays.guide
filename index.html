Monaco, our way.

Not quite a guidebook. More like a local's map.

Monaco is small enough to cross in an afternoon, but there is considerably more to discover than the Casino and the harbour. We have gathered the gardens, viewpoints, little streets, hidden corners and beautiful places we return to ourselves.

Gardens & Green Spaces

01
01 · Garden

Jardins Saint-Martin

Monaco's first public garden, winding along the cliffs above the Mediterranean. Beautifully quiet in the morning.

02 · Garden

Japanese Garden

A surprisingly peaceful pocket beside the sea, designed around water, stone, bridges and carefully composed greenery.

03

Jardin Exotique

Dramatic succulents and panoramic views from Monaco's upper slopes.

04

Princess Grace Rose Garden

Nearly 10,000 roses in a peaceful corner of Fontvieille.

05

Parc Princesse Antoinette

A quieter, greener side of Monaco that most visitors never reach.

By the Sea

02
06 · Beach

Larvotto

Monaco's public beach and waterfront promenade. Go early for a completely different atmosphere.

07 · Hidden corner

Plage des Pêcheurs

A tiny rocky waterfront tucked beneath the old town. One of the places that feels surprisingly far from the city.

08

Port Hercule

Walk the harbour rather than simply looking at it from above.

09

Port de Fontvieille

A calmer harbour to wander around at golden hour.

10

Monte-Carlo Beach

A classic Riviera setting just beyond the eastern edge of Monaco.

Our little tip

Larvotto before 9am is another place entirely. The water is calm, the promenade is quiet and Monaco has not quite started its day yet.

A different perspective

Look up. Monaco goes much higher than you think.

One of the pleasures of Monaco is discovering how vertical it is. Take the lifts, climb the small passages and follow the streets above the centre for unexpected views across the rooftops and sea.

The Rock & Monaco-Ville

03
11

Monaco-Ville

Wander without a route. The old streets of the Rock are at their best when you simply follow whichever staircase looks interesting.

12

Place du Palais

The ceremonial heart of Monaco, with views stretching across the rooftops and Mediterranean.

13

Rampe Major

The historic approach to the Rock — best explored slowly.

14

Fort Antoine

An unusual little open-air theatre overlooking the sea. One of Monaco's most atmospheric corners.

15

Cathedral & Gardens

Visit the cathedral, then continue directly into the Saint-Martin gardens above the sea.

Our little tip

Visit Monaco-Ville before the day gets busy. Around 8:30–9:00am, the narrow streets feel almost private.

Art, Architecture & Culture

04
16

Casino de Monte-Carlo

Belle Époque architecture and one of the most recognisable interiors on the Riviera.

17

Salle Garnier

The intimate opera house of Monte-Carlo, built in the same architectural spirit as the Casino.

18

Villa Paloma

Contemporary art in one of Monaco's elegant historic villas.

19

Villa Sauber

The Belle Époque home of the Nouveau Musée National de Monaco.

20

Grimaldi Forum

Monaco's contemporary cultural centre for exhibitions, performances and major events.

21

Prince's Car Collection

A fascinating collection for anyone interested in automotive history and Monaco's royal heritage.

The Little Discoveries

05
22 · Viewpoint

Tête de Chien

High above Monaco, with one of the Riviera's most extraordinary views back towards the Principality.

23 · Walk

Chemin des Révoires

A quieter climb through Monaco's upper neighbourhoods, with beautiful views appearing between the buildings.

24 · Evening

Le Rocher at Sunset

Forget the itinerary. Walk towards the western edge of the Rock and watch the city change colour.

25

Fontvieille Sculpture Trail

A quieter side of Monaco with gardens, sculpture and plenty of space to simply wander.

26

Public Lifts of Monaco

One of the city's best-kept practical secrets. Use the lifts to move vertically through Monaco and discover different neighbourhoods.

27

Larvotto at Dawn

Before the beach becomes busy, the promenade is calm, quiet and almost cinematic.

Beyond Monaco

06

When you have explored the Principality, the Riviera begins immediately. These are the places we would choose for a half-day escape.

10 minutes

Cap d'Ail

A beautiful coastal village directly beside Monaco. Follow the waterfront and discover the dramatic coastline.

10 minutes

Plage Mala

A spectacular little cove beneath the cliffs of Cap d'Ail.

15 minutes

Roquebrune-Cap-Martin

Medieval streets, sea views and a completely different atmosphere just east of Monaco.

15 minutes

Beaulieu-sur-Mer

Elegant Riviera architecture, palms and beautiful waterfront walks.

20 minutes

Saint-Jean-Cap-Ferrat

One of the most beautiful peninsulas on the Côte d'Azur, especially for a slow afternoon by the sea.

20 minutes

Villefranche-sur-Mer

A postcard-perfect bay with colourful façades and a wonderful waterfront promenade.

25 minutes

Èze Village

Medieval stone streets perched high above the Mediterranean.

30 minutes

Menton

Pastel façades, gardens and a distinctly Italian feeling at the eastern edge of the Riviera.

30 minutes

Nice

The Promenade des Anglais, Old Town and the extraordinary views from Castle Hill.
